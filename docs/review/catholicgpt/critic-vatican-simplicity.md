## Remaining defects

1. Home still repeats the Gospel reference in the excerpt and the “Gospel of the day” row. That lower row may have a useful meditation action, but fold the action into the existing Gospel section or make its purpose clear without repeating the citation. The extra rule and blank gap above “Read today's Mass” also add unnecessary ceremony.
2. Bible and Prayers retain an oversized empty band between search and filters and another between filters and content. Reduce these gaps. Tiny tracked capital filter names remain harder to scan than the body labels; reduce tracking and increase their size/contrast. Prayers' right-edge filter continuation needs a clearer affordance if it scrolls.
3. Rosary's Instructions button sits between a full-width divider and two short rules: three lines for one secondary action. Remove the redundant framing. The bead visualization is relevant, but still dominates the viewport; a slightly smaller diagram could reveal the mystery selector fully without scrolling.
4. Dark inactive navigation labels, chevrons and disabled-looking previous arrow remain faint. Confirm intended disabled states and increase contrast for available controls. Screenshots do not establish measured accessibility compliance.

No screenshot-visible blocker prevents preferring the simpler candidates. They are good, disciplined progress; scores remain below exceptional because small-control legibility and residual spacing/framing still need refinement. This is a visual evaluation, not confirmation of implementation architecture or interaction behavior.
