## Three priority corrections

1. **Redraw or retune artwork for each palette.** The cross uses a large fan of mechanically repeated rays and dense nested linework; the book has heavy outlines/hatching; candle halos form an overly busy cluster. Use fewer deliberate strokes, clearer silhouettes and a consistent line hierarchy. In dark mode reduce the almost white cross/book outlines and coral book fill: text and primary controls should retain the strongest contrast. Preserve the images, but treat them as crafted frontispieces rather than glowing badges.
2. **Restore practical vertical balance on Home.** Retain the cross and feast panel, but reduce the art region and gaps around Today's Mass/Reflection enough to expose at least two daily prayer rows. The feast panel's pale rectangular fill, purple left edge and gold bottom rule are three competing treatments; keep one restrained boundary plus the seasonal rubric. Bible and Prayers also have a large search-to-selector gap that can shrink without sacrificing elegance.
3. **Make small UI typography and actions confidently readable.** Inactive navigation captions, dark purple seasonal text, gold chevrons and mystery Change text are too subdued. Increase secondary contrast and caption size, reduce excessive tracking at tiny sizes, and remove redundant framing around Rosary Instructions (full divider plus short top/bottom rules). Give Font's current value a readable named family if known, rather than only an implementation-like default label.
