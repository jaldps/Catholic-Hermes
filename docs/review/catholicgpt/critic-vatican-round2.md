## Three concrete remaining defects

1. **Home's revised sunburst is a regression.** It overlaps title/date and the feast block, turning negative space into visual noise. Clip or constrain rays to a dedicated illustration region, retain the useful Mass action, and set “Violet · Commemoration” as smaller secondary metadata instead of a third title line. Home's strongest visual moment should have disciplined boundaries.
2. **Small secondary typography is too weak, especially in dark mode.** Navigation captions, italic list descriptions, tiny gold filter labels and chevrons require effort. Raise secondary text contrast, increase navigation caption size while reducing tracking, and make inactive arrows more discernible. Preserve subdued rules and ornaments so the text gains hierarchy rather than the whole screen becoming brighter.
3. **Utility screens still waste space and filter affordances are unresolved.** Bible/Prayers have large masthead-to-task areas even after shrinking art, then another sizable gap between filters and section titles. Reduce those gaps and make the filter row's horizontal continuation clear: Prayers currently presents “Peace of Mind” against the right edge without a visible continuation cue. Show enough useful list content to justify keeping the decorative masthead.

The successful revision is a meaningful usability improvement, especially Rosary. The giant-ray Home variant prevents a blanket endorsement of the revised set. The identity remains clearly liturgical and book-inspired rather than a generic skin, but the combination of ornamental collisions and small low-contrast UI text keeps it below the requested bar.
