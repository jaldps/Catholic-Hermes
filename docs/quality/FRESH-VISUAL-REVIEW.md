## Craft assessment

- **Lumen:** Consistent rounded geometry and generous type make the pages approachable. The header fades and neutral surfaces are coherent. However, large same-elevation white/charcoal cards and heavily shadowed capsule controls give a conventional component-library character. The dock is easy to read but appears essentially opaque; do not describe these images as proof of sophisticated glass rendering. Reserve depth for the control layer and vary content pacing rather than adding more shadows.
- **Sanctuary:** Stronger editorial character. Warm ground, framed landscape/flowers, literary headings, oxblood actions and underlined solid navigation work together. Light and dark stay related without harsh inversion. Bible recommendation remains a deeply rounded image card while surrounding components are tighter; align this featured-card geometry with the identity if it is not an intentional shared exception. Rosary is the main coherence exception.
- **Typography:** All inspected root labels and controls are legible at this size, with useful sans/serif separation. No visible root heading collision or text clipping. The tiny scripture source sentence is nevertheless disproportionately prominent because of placement and length. No loaded font files or measured font sizes were inspected by this reviewer.
- **Spacing:** Root pages align to common gutters and have calm inter-section spacing. Home's three-action stack and the Rosary header have the clearest space-budget problems. Do not remove all breathing room globally to address these local issues.
- **Navigation occlusion:** Partly hidden category rows, book rows and settings rows are normal at the bottom of a scroll viewport and are not proof of unreachable content. The prayer fade actively reduces readable content and requires the explicit final-line scroll check above. Lower-scroll captures are required before accepting dock clearance.
- **Beginner clarity:** Today, Pray (`Pray the Lord’s Prayer`) and Explore (`Take a first step`) supply a clear entry. Scripture and Rosary depend too much on unlabeled media icon interpretation. The Pray screen's guided Rosary description is useful and should remain.
