# Catholic pastoral content review — 13 November 2026

## Evidence and scope

Reviewed the current checkout, `AGENTS.md`, content-integrity/provenance notes, quality delivery/secondary/critic notes, current moral and temptation implementations, the seven vice/virtue datasets, the daily rule, the Ignatian examen, calendar and reading-source handling. Opened existing `habits_virtues_lumen_light.png`, `spiritual_life_sanctuary_light.png`, and `pilgrimage_habits_sanctuary_light.png` in `app/build/screenshots/app`. Those dated November 12 frames establish their visible content only; they do not validate the concurrent November 13 changes. No Gradle run, live app interaction or ecclesiastical approval is claimed by this review.

The app already has substantial useful content: all seven capital vices have countervirtues and practical advice; temptation offers the Hail Mary, Saint Michael and Act of Contrition; the moral section explains the three conditions for mortal sin, diminished responsibility, perfect contrition and the Communion rule; Confession has a five-step outline; the Ignatian examen begins with gratitude and explicitly avoids scrupulous condemnation. The full Catechism has source provenance. These are existing strengths, not missing features.

The main pastoral weakness is the distance between that careful teaching and the moment someone presses “I fell.” A counter records a person's recollection; it cannot determine culpability, sacramental forgiveness or holiness. More content volume is less valuable than making the existing guidance immediately usable.

## Eight bounded priorities

1. **Put the distinction between temptation and sin beside the record controls. Implemented by the temptation owner; source rechecked.** Being tempted is distinct from consenting (CCC 2847). The new ES/EN/PT note says recording is optional and counts do not judge guilt or holiness. Keep thanksgiving and beginning again as the purpose of the two records. Do not derive moral verdicts, success percentages or spiritual ranks from them. The existing statistics/calendar are present; the improvement is their framing. For someone whose counting fuels scrupulous anxiety, a brief optional help note can encourage setting it aside and following a trusted confessor's guidance. That is a pastoral recommendation, not an automated diagnosis.

2. **Connect the fall sheet to the existing Sin and Confession guidance. Implemented by the temptation owner; source rechecked.** The reassuring invitation to contrition and Confession is good. The new localized text clarifies that reciting a prayer is not sacramental absolution and directs uncertainty to a priest. A real act of perfect contrition has the conditions already explained in the moral page; do not compress it into “tap this to be forgiven.” Preserve the existing explanation that awareness of grave sin ordinarily calls for sacramental absolution before Communion, with the precise exception. CCC 1451–1453, 1457 and canon 916 support this distinction. The new internal-link callback should land at `grav:0`, which contains that fuller explanation; the three-condition page is `grav:1`.

3. **Use source-close moral language without inviting a certainty test. Implemented in this review.** The Spanish condition previously asked whether the person knew “con certeza.” All three localized condition summaries now follow CCC 1859's knowledge of the act's sinful character and opposition to God's law. The three conditions, diminished-responsibility explanation and referral to a priest remain. This is a precision improvement, not a claim that the former sentence invalidated the whole teaching. CCC 1861 also expressly distinguishes judging an act from judging a person.

4. **Make the existing virtue remedies compatible with health and real human limits. Implemented in this review.** Both the doctrine advice and emergency catalog now offer a manageable next step for acedia, distinguish fatigue/illness from sloth, and acknowledge rest/help. Temperance advice preserves necessary nourishment, makes sacrifice compatible with health and offers service as an alternative to food restriction. This applies CCC 1735, 1860 and 2288–2290; it does not diagnose a user or abolish legitimate fasting discipline. The English Marian resolution now says “by God's grace and with your prayers,” making the source of grace and Mary's intercession clear (CCC 969–970). This is devotional clarity, not an accusation that every use of “your grace” is heretical.

5. **Clarify the daily rule's status and distinguish reading from Mass. Implemented by the habits owner; source rechecked.** Twelve existing practices are a suggested personal rule; a missed devotional checkbox is not automatically a sin. The “Holy Mass / Daily readings” label can blur distinct acts. The new ES/EN/PT footnote calls this a suggested personal rule and says reading Scripture does not itself fulfill the Sunday Mass obligation. Canons 1247–1248 also address genuine impossibility; local pastoral guidance belongs in linked help.

6. **Make the existing five-step Confession outline useful to a beginner.** Add a short “What do I say?” example, permission to tell the priest that help is needed, and a calm explanation of confessing remembered grave sins in kind and number after reasonable examination (canon 988; CCC 1454–1456). Explain absolution and the assigned penance, and connect amendment with repairing harm where possible (CCC 1459). The Spanish “examen detallado” should become a sincere, reasonable examination; completeness should not be presented as endless reconstruction. Keep the existing five-step structure and Act of Contrition instead of building another parallel flow.

7. **Move truthful liturgical provenance to the point of reading.** The repository already labels bundled passages as reading editions, not an approved liturgical lectionary; the feed parser already requires the requested date. Preserve those repairs. The inspected `BibleScreen` fallback banner still says “Offline · readings from the bundled lectionary,” and its empty heading says “Ferial day without proper readings.” Prefer “Online readings unavailable · bundled Scripture selections” and “Today's readings are unavailable here.” A failed feed is not proof that all connectivity is offline, and a missing local entry does not mean the Church appoints no weekday readings. A short General Roman Calendar/local-variation note is also useful where the app displays the day; the computation already avoids universal claims of local holy-day obligation. Canons 1246 §2 and 1253 support the local distinction. Exact banner copy has been sent to root.

8. **Curate the existing library before adding more devotions.** Source notes already acknowledge that the 246 inherited novenas lack complete original provenance. Prioritize a small, frequently used set: record source, whether text is traditional or editorial, language review, and any permission basis; link to the official text where available. Similarly, add a concise Scripture/CCC link to an existing vice/virtue explanation rather than another disconnected list. The seven remedies and prayers already exist. Greater trust and a connected “understand → practice → pray” path would help more than a larger catalog.
