CHAPLET OF
SAINT KATERI TEKAKWITHA
(Also known as: the Kateri Indian Rosary)
How to pray the Chaplet of Saint Kateri Tekakwitha
This Chaplet has 24 beads, each bead representing one year of Saint Kateri's holy life.
There are 8 brown beads (the colour of the earth),
8 red beads (the color of blood),
and 8 crystal beads (clear like water).
On each brown bead, say the "Our Father".
On each red bead, say the "Hail Mary".
On each crystal bead, say the "Glory be".
