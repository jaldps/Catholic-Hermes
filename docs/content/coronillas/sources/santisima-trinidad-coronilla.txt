CHAPLET OF THE
MOST HOLY TRINITY
How to pray the Chaplet of the Most Holy Trinity
The Most Holy Trinity chaplet is normally recited on the regular Rosary.
It should be offered through the Five Sacred Wounds of Our Lord in reparation
for the crimes of sinners.
You begin by saying on the Crucifix,
"Incline unto mine aid, O God,
O Lord make hast to help me."
On the three small and one large bead, you say,
"Glory be to Thee,
O most sweet, most noble,
resplendent, peaceful,
ineffable Trinity.
Amen
On each of the ten small beads of each decade, you say,
"Glory, honor and praise be to the great Eternal God. Amen.
Then you say the Glory Be To The Father
after each decade of 10 small beads.
You conclude the chaplet with three more Glory Be To The Father
and the recitation of the following Divine Praises,
Blessed be God!
Blessed be his Holy Name!
Blessed be Jesus Christ, true God and true man!
Blessed be the Name of Jesus!
Blessed be his Most Sacred Heart!
Blessed be his Most Precious Blood!
Blessed be Jesus in the Most Holy Sacrament of the Altar!
Blessed be the Holy Spirit, the Paraclete!
Blessed be the great Mother of God, Mary most Holy!
Blessed be her holy and Immaculate Conception!
Blessed be her glorious Assumption!
Blessed be the name of Mary, Virgin and Mother!
Blessed be St. Joseph, her most chaste spouse!
Blessed be God in his Angels and his Saints!
