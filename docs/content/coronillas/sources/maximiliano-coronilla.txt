CHAPLET OF
SAINT MAXIMILLIAN KOLBE
How to pray the Chaplet of St. Maximillian
This Chaplet has a Medal of St. Maximillian, 1 bead,
1 Medal of the Virgin Mary and 14 beads as follows:
Medal + 1 + Medal + 14.
Begin with the Memorare Prayer on the
single bead between the 2 Medals.
On each of the 14 beads, say the Miraculous Medal Prayer followed by the
invocation to St. Maximillian as follows:
Prayer
"O Mary, conceived without sin,
pray for us who have recourse to Thee,
and for all those who do not have recourse to Thee,
especially for the enemies of Holy Mother Church,
and all those in most need of Thy mercy."
Invocation
"St. Maximillian,
pray for us!"
