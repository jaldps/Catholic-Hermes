CHAPLET OF
SAINT ANNE
How to pray the Chaplet of Saint Anne
The chaplet to St. Anne promises a great number of spiritual and temporal favors.
The Chaplet of good Saint Anne consist of the following prayers.
1. In honour of Jesus,
Say 1 Our Father...
and 5 Hail Mary's.
Then say,
"Jesus, Mary and St. Anne,
grant the favour I ask."
2. In honour of Mary,
Say 1 Our Father...
and 5 Hail Mary's.
Then say,
"Jesus, Mary and St. Anne,
grant the favour I ask."
3. In honour of Saint Anne,
Say 1 Our Father...
and 5 Hail Mary's.
Then say,
"Jesus, and St. Anne,
grant the favour I ask."
