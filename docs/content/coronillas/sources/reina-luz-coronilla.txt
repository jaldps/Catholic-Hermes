THE CHAPLET OF THE
QUEEN OF LIGHT
How to pray the Chaplet of the Queen of Light
The Chaplet of the Queen of Light consists of a Sacred Heart Medal
and 18 beads that are divided in 3 sets of 6 beads each.
On the first bead of each set of six, reflect on the meditation (found below)
and pray one "Our Father...".
On the other five beads of the set, pray the "Hail Mary".
First Meditation
A thousand promises are not worth one small, meaningful prayer.
Second Meditation
"My child, it is necessary to keep close to My Divine Son,
especially in the Blessed Sacrament."
Third Meditation
"I am the Mother of the hopeless;
Mother of Sorrows.
It is important for those that are suffering
to come to me for consolation and for comfort."
