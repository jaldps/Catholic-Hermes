CHAPLET OF
SAINT FRANCIS XAVIER CABRINI
How to pray the Chaplet of Saint Frances Xavier Cabrini
This Chaplet has 25 beads, 5 sets of 5 beads, and a medal of Mother Cabrini.
On each of the beads, say the "Hail Mary".
At the end of each set of 5 beads, say the following prayer:
"O My Saviour,
hear Saint Frances Xavier Cabrini
plead for me.
